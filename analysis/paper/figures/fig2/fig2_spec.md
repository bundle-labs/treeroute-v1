- **Style.** Light blue, dark blue and orange for the three windows (adjacent pairs separated under simulated protan and deutan vision and in greyscale; checked with a Python port of the dataviz validator because node is not installed); hatch for the left-out 2020 visits; black cut lines; text in ink, never in a series colour; Arial for text, DejaVu Sans italic for Greek and italic math (Arial's italic tau reads as T, the glossary's tree-answer symbol); cut symbols 8.5 pt with mathtext's script factor raised so that every sub- and superscript is 7.0 pt; no text below 7 pt at print size; vector PDF (fonts embedded) plus 300-dpi PNG. fig2.py fails if a panel (b) line overflows its box, a left-column line runs into the plot, a label runs off the page or two panel (a) labels overlap.
- **Do not.** Draw calendar lock dates (P17 gives their order; Supplement S4 lists the times; the figure is dateless by design); show any score, Part number, ruling letter, signature detail or hash; say "pre-registered" or "leave-one-region-out"; call coral bleaching's window "the last four years"; mark one problem as untouched by its test window; say a test-exposed choice was caused by the test window.
