**Figure 1.** The study regions and the coral bleaching reef sites. Each of the 133 panel regions is shown as the area that its locations cover on a 0.25° grid, clipped to the coastline. All of them are NDVI regions; 127 are also PM2.5 regions, 40 are burned-fraction regions and 42 are forest-loss regions. Colour and line texture indicate which of the last two problems use a region: both burned fraction and forest loss (13 regions; blue, crossed lines), burned fraction only (27; orange, lines rising to the right), forest loss only (29; aqua, lines falling to the right) or neither (64; grey, no lines). Open outlines mark the 6 polar or edge regions that have no PM2.5 because its whole-region coverage rule leaves them out (named in Supplement S1.5). The dots are the 8,131 reef sites of the 83 coral bleaching regions, each drawn once. They are filled for the 7,065 reef sites in the 37 scored regions and open for the 1,066 in the 46 training-only regions; the 26 reef sites of the 11 excluded regions are not drawn. The regions are drawn on top of the reef sites and cover the centres of 229 of them, 188 of which are in south Florida (Supplement S9.3). The projection is equirectangular, from 60°S to 80°N. Coastlines are from GSHHG 2.3.7, low resolution (Wessel and Smith 1996), distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later.
