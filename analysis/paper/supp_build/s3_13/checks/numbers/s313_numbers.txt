=== seg 9
VIS: **Method.** Each model is explained in its own link space by SHAP values (Lundberg and Lee 2017), taken as interventional Shapley values (Janzing et al. 2020): a set of players is worth the mean, over the explained row's background rows, of the model's output with those players at the explained row's values and every other player at the background row's.
TOKENS: ['2017', '2020']
COMMENT: A10; SHAP protocol section 5 (value function, decision D3)

=== seg 18
VIS: A player's importance to a model, $I_m(j)$, is its mean absolute SHAP value over the sample rows, with each region weighted equally; a model's ranking orders the players by importance, ties broken by name. Two kinds of share are used. A player's share is its importance over the model's total importance over J, the base of every top-10 and top-3 share. A family's share is its mean absolute attribution, with each region weighted equally, over the sum of the five families' mean absolute attributions, a family's attribution on a row being the sum of its variables' SHAP values; the own-history share is thus a family share, not the sum of the own-history variables' shares. J, at each level, holds the players to which some model gives more than 10⁻¹² of its total mean absolute SHAP value; every other player, a structural zero, is exactly 0 on every row of every model, so a plain mean would give the same J.
TOKENS: ['10', '3', '10', '0']
COMMENT: A10

=== seg 53
VIS: **Not claimed.** SHAP values show what a model uses to move its forecast away from its background, not what causes the outcome. Under B2 they describe how a test-window forecast differs from the same location's forecasts with the same season key in the training years: a column that equals the explained row on every background row is 0 by construction, not unused, and columns with a trend take credit for level shifts; on coral bleaching, whose background is a pool of earlier nearby survey visits, static and calendar columns can carry attribution. Agreement between rankings is not correctness: models that agree may lean on the same proxies. Cross-link pairs are not comparable in scale or rank, so no comparison is made across links. A zero does not mean that an input is irrelevant: correlated inputs split the attribution arbitrarily (the variable level reduces this), and the interventional values evaluate each model on rows that mix the explained row's values with a background row's, combinations that may never occur. BARDL reads nine columns by design, three lags of the location's own label and precipitation and soil moisture at the issue step and the two steps before (published inputs only; ruling AB), so its zeros elsewhere mean "not an input"; it counts in the NDVI main set, as the frozen plan has it (ruling BA1), its top-k sets follow name order beyond its inputs, and its per-location coefficients are not reported, as its map files stay out of the archive (ruling AV6).
TOKENS: ['0']
COMMENT: A15
