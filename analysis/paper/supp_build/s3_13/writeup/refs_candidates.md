## Entries (section A style; one line each)

- **Janzing, D., Minorics, L., & Blöbaum, P. (2020).** Feature relevance quantification in explainable AI: A causal problem. In *Proceedings of the Twenty Third International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics* (Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, Vol. 108, pp. 2907–2916). PMLR. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v108/janzing20a.html — Key `Janzing2020_interventional`. Supports: the interventional (baseline) value function of the SHAP comparison (Supplement S3.13, method paragraph): a coalition's value is the mean, over the explained row's background rows, of the model's link-space output with the coalition's players at the row's values and every other player at the background row's. The paper argues that the features a coalition leaves out should be averaged over their own distribution, not conditioned on the kept ones (its eq. 4, against the conditional form of its eq. 3, which it attributes to Lundberg and Lee 2017), and shows that this average is the interventional expectation in Pearl's sense (its eq. 14). The per-row background is our choice, not the paper's (B2: the same location's router fit rows of the same calendar month; forest loss, annual: all of them; coral bleaching: a pool of earlier nearby survey visits): say so where it is cited. Check (2026-11-17, S3.13 write-up): PMLR's page and the BibTeX it gives confirm the title, the three authors, the proceedings title, volume 108 of Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, pp. 2907–2916, 2020, the editors (Silvia Chiappa and Roberto Calandra) and the publisher, PMLR; they spell the third author "Bloebaum". The PDF that the page links (read 2026-11-17), the arXiv record of the preprint (1910.13413v2 of 27 November 2019, same title; arXiv API, read 2026-11-17) and the reference list of Lundberg et al. 2020 spell him Blöbaum, as the entry does. The PDF's first-page footer names AISTATS 2020, Palermo, Italy, and PMLR volume 108; eqs. 3, 4 and 14 were read in it. No DOI.
