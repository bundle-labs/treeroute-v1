2. THE RULE, EXACTLY (AE1 + amendment), AS CODE WILL READ IT
=====================================================================================================
2.1 Names. Adopted arm A = (link, tool, weighting) of the pass:
      nowcast: Adoption.adopted (adoption.json). If the nowcast DECLINED: status not_applicable_declined.
      lead L:  the pinned arm when the lead's guard passed (LeadAdoption.adopted). If the guard failed
               (the lead adopts the decline target): status not_applicable_declined.
2.2 The twin T (generic, from the arm alone; layer/alarm.py twin_of):
      (a) the WEIGHTING twin = same link, same tool, the OTHER weighting -- used when that weighting is
          routed in the pass's kind (weightings(): GLS exists for per-unit and neighbour, not for region);
      (b) else the TOOL twin = same link, same weighting, plain <-> corrector.
    T is "simpler" (layer/alarm.py twin_is_simpler) iff T is unweighted while A is GLS (case a), or T is
    plain while A is corrector (case b). Otherwise the alarm is INERT for the pass: status
    inert_twin_not_simpler; nothing can switch.
    What this gives on the five v1 adoptions (read from their adoption.json today):
      coral  arcsine_corrector_gls        -> twin arcsine_corrector_unweighted (simpler)  can fire
      fire   arcsine_corrector_gls        -> twin arcsine_corrector_unweighted (simpler)  can fire
      defo   log_c_corrector_unweighted (region kind, no GLS) -> twin log_c_plain_unweighted (simpler) can fire
      pm25   log_c_corrector_unweighted (per-unit) -> twin log_c_corrector_gls (NOT simpler) inert
      ndvi   identity_plain_unweighted (per-unit)  -> twin identity_plain_gls  (NOT simpler) inert
    (See OWNER DECISION D1: the literal reading above vs "pick the simpler neighbour".)
2.3 Twin eligible (layer/alarm.py twin_eligible):
      nowcast: T is in Adoption.eligible (the layer's own N2 / V1 test on the decision surface, 1.4).
      lead L:  on the lead's GUARD surface, MAE(T at the pinned point) < the same baseline the lead's own
               guard used: its link's trees-off MAE (grid; ladder.py 824-825), or the donor-row median MAE
               under V1 (table; table_ladder.py 354-358). T shares A's link, so that number is already
               computed by the guard. Strict "<", the same n2_eligible (decide.py 290).
      Not eligible -> status inert_twin_not_eligible.
2.4 The reference gap R (one number per pass, computed BEFORE labels open, from label-free predictions):
      nowcast: mean |A - T| over the decision-surface rows, both columns at the ADOPTED point, clipped
               (inner_preds.parquet columns "<A>@<point>" and "<T>@<point>").
      lead L:  mean |A - T| over the lead's GUARD-surface rows at the pinned point, clipped (the guard is the
               lead's own decision surface: R29 decides the lead there, so its scale of disagreement is the
               lead's own; the nowcast surface is at another horizon). See OWNER DECISION D2.
      If R is not finite or R == 0: status not_tested_zero_reference_gap (recorded and printed; nothing
      switches; a ratio over zero means nothing).
2.5 Batches (AE1 amendment; layer/alarm.py batch_keys, generic, no problem names):
      the batch of a test row is the calendar month of its ISSUE, i.e. the saved column "issue" of
      test_preds (time - lead):
        - a grid whose step covers one calendar month or more (monthly, annual): the batch is the issue STEP
          itself (monthly -> one month; annual -> the issue year), key "step:<n>";
        - anything finer (composite-of-year steps; table/event rows dated by day ordinals): the calendar
          month of the issue's first day (io/steps.py step_bounds 388 for steps; date.fromordinal for days),
          key "YYYY-MM".
      Rows counted = every saved test row of the pass in that batch (after S1 and H4 left rows out), with or
      without a label (labels are unknown at that moment). A batch with fewer than ALARM_MIN_BATCH_ROWS (30)
      rows is not tested: the adopted arm stands for it (tested=false).
2.6 Per tested batch b: g_b = mean |A - T| over the batch's rows (clipped test predictions, the same
    arm__ values the pass saves); ratio_b = g_b / R. FIRES iff ratio_b > ALARM_RATIO_THRESHOLD (2.0, strict)
    AND the pass status is active (twin simpler AND eligible AND R finite > 0). A fired batch's rows take T's
    prediction in the "with alarm" column; every other row keeps A's. The adopted column itself is never
    changed.
2.7 Statuses (one per pass): active | inert_twin_not_simpler | inert_twin_not_eligible |
    not_applicable_declined | not_tested_zero_reference_gap. Only "active" can fire. The with-alarm column
    exists for EVERY pass (equal to the adopted column when nothing fires), so with/without is always
    reportable (AE2).
