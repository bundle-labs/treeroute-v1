3. CALIBRATION MAPS AND THE PICK (SPEC_HUMAN 1.6; PROT H6; Q19 a; Q2.HQ6 a)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  F0 pred = x | F1 link(y) = a + b link(x) (x put in the link domain first) | F2 link(y) = iso(x) |
  F3 link(y) - z0 = a + b (x - xbar) | F4 link(y) - z0 = iso(x - xbar) | F5 per unit: link(y) - z0 = a_u + b_u
  (x - xbar), shrunk to the pooled F3 with SHRINK_K = 10 rows: s* = (n_u s_u + K s_pool) / (n_u + K); a unit with
  < 3 covered fit rows (or unseen) takes the pooled map; a unit with no x spread keeps the pooled slope (counted).
  z0 = the row's link-space normal (guard-cut on tune / inner / guard-fit / guard rows; fit-cut on refit and test
  rows). xbar = the index mean per unit x season key on the same fit rows (< 3 rows: pooled key mean; unseen key:
  pooled mean).
  Isotonic direction = the sign of Spearman of the PAIR THE MAP FITS on its fit rows: F2 x vs y; F4 (and the
  anomaly L1 map) x - xbar vs link(y) - z0 (undefined -> increasing). (Fixed 2026-10-22: F4 took the raw pair.)
  sklearn IsotonicRegression (clip out of bounds), squared error, knots kept. No weights. Back-transform with
  link_inverse, no smearing; clip to the legal range, pre-clip kept.
  Minimum to fit a map: >= 100 covered labelled rows in >= 3 groups, else "not calibratable at this lead" (no row,
  disclosed) - except on a lead scored without the safety net (section 5).
  Candidates = stats (<= 3) x families x the run's R22 links, fixed order (stat, F0..F6, link). Pick = the lowest
  MAE of the clipped prediction on the pick rows covered by every stat; ties by the fixed order. Nowcast rivals:
  fit on tune rows, picked on lead-0 inner rows. Forecast-only products: fit on guard-fit rows, picked on the guard
  rows of their lowest covered lead. One pick per rival, pinned at every lead, refit per lead on the lead's refit
  rows. Runners (bardl_h1, kraft_lstm) have one configuration, no pick. corr2, calibration ratio and predict-zero
  MAE are logged beside every candidate (trials.csv).
  L1 print (Q13): where the run's legal minimum is 0 and the lead-0 refit zero share is >= 0.20 (data rule; coral
  and defo), the pick's L1 isotonic map is refit and printed beside (l1_raw_vs_raw), never decides. For an F0 pick
  (vt0007_shipped, vt0007_frozen2019) it is labelled "L1 isotonic calibration of the as-shipped index".

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Coverage (HQ3 a): the test rows the pick covers, fixed at the lock and hashed: finite picked stat in the index
  (one-go index tables) or a label-free registry rule (staged tables and runners): coral x_finite; defo
  {time_min: 2011} (target year >= first loss year + HRP = the adapter's own rule at every lead; fixed 2026-10-22,
  was issue_min 2010); PM2.5 x_finite; NDVI H1 {units_column_true: is_scored}; Kraft is_scored + a label-free window
  function. A staged index must later cover every locked row, else refit refuses.
  Safety net (HQ2 a): lead 0 guard = the pick fitted on tune rows, scored on its covered inner rows (and checked to
  reproduce its tuning predictions byte for byte); lead L = fitted on covered guard-fit rows, scored on covered guard
  rows. Comparator on the same rows: the run's saved <link>_trees_off_unweighted of the pick's link (F0 and runners:
  identity), or the donor median (neighbour kind, V1). Passed iff MAE(rival) < MAE(comparator) strictly. Guarded =
  raw if passed, else the run's decline target. Both raw and guarded are scored; guarded decides.
  Leads scored without the safety net (coral crw_outlook at 365, Q12 b): guarded = raw, flagged "no safety net";
  if the guard map cannot even be fitted there (expected: ~38 guard-fit rows / 11 groups), the guard is NOT RUN,
  flagged, and the lead is still refit on its refit rows and scored raw. (Fixed 2026-10-22: such a lead lost its
  row.)
  Deciding contrast: covered rows of groups with >= 10 covered test rows, ours on the same rows; d_g = MAE_rival(g) -
  MAE_ours(g); the engine's contrast_tag with the run's nowcast band at every lead: BEATS / LOSES / EQUIVALENT /
  CANNOT TELL; blocked jackknife beside; timid flag. guarded_vs_headline decides (P, L); raw_vs_raw printed.
  Beside each row: rows covered of all rows ("on the k% of rows the product covers", k = rows_covered / rows_total),
  deciding rows, groups, years, our all-row MAE, the guess-normal MAE on the same rows, anomaly R2, corr2, hit rate,
  a "filled" line (never decides), per-stratum and unseen-unit tables, resolution-matched tables for coarse products.
  Wording: a tier-L BEATS is worded with its label ("beats NOAA's nowcast rule fed old heat"), never "beats NOAA".
  A tier-X row that is not a placebo is a "labelled statistical extension"; a placebo is a guard check, never a
  rival.

10. DISCLOSURES (from the registries; the 2026-10-22 additions marked NEW)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  General: human rivals sit on the same saved test rows, cuts, normals and metrics as the common board; all fits on
  train rows only; nothing is added after a human test score.
  Coral: the rival is a subset of our inputs (same CoralTemp SST, NOAA's MMM): it measures what the model adds over
  NOAA's rule; MMM content runs to 2012; CoralTemp is a reanalysis to Oct 2016, NRT after; the v3.1 suite (2018) is
  later than the 2016-17 issue dates; 5 alert levels (later levels 5-7 merged into 4); d* = C - g - 1; units are
  visits, so F5 falls back to the pooled map (counted). Outlook: experimental; the files are a 2019 v5 reissue;
  0.5 deg cells, a land cell takes the MAXIMUM alert of the nearest ocean ring (favours the Outlook); train coverage
  from 2011-11-14; 365 d has no safety net (Q12 b) and, if its guard map cannot be fitted, no guard at all (NEW).
  Defo: the rival sees the 30 m forest map (treecover2000 + past loss) our model does not (Q15); quantity from
  wall-to-wall Hansen counts; annual rolling use of a certified frozen method is our adaptation; anisotropic pixel
  spacing per block; a fixed 512-bin log distance grid (NRT snapped up ~2%); GADM 3.6 level 2 (content 2018), IRL
  and HKG level 1, cell -> unit by largest overlap; GFC pre-2011 regime break and reprocessing look-ahead (shared
  with the target); the calibration map is fitted on train rows only. NEW: info_end = 2000 and GADM is treated as
  fixed geography (section 4); Hansen loss of year t-1 is usually published in the spring of year t+1, after the
  issue date: generous to the rival, as our own t-1 labels are.
  PM2.5: EAC4 is published 4-6 months late (t-1 is generous); EAC4 and MERRA-2 assimilate AOD, the target's own
  source, so the P rows are lagged to t-1; EAC4 holds no nitrate (verify); MERRA-2's formula has no nitrate; 5
  MERRA-2 test months are the reprocessed MERRA2_401 stream; same-month rows break T1/T2 by design (tier A). NEW:
  MERRA-2 of month t-1 may not be published at the issue date (generous to the rival).
  NDVI: H1 works on the NDVI anomaly (normal = the engine's identity normal), not VCI; BARDL is published for 6-10
  weeks, piloted in 7 Kenyan counties, so leads 6/12 are an extension; BayesianRidge defaults; missing NDVI lags or
  climate values at prediction set to anomaly 0, counted. NEW: at lead 0 H1 reads the TARGET month's precipitation
  and soil moisture (R8 steps 0; our model reads them too: parity, not a leak), so "only past rain" is not literally
  true. Kraft: research model, not operational; reads no NDVI; land cover dropped (banned proxy).
