4.1 adapters/fire_enso.py: ENSO-lag regression (Chen et al. 2011, 2016). OWNER DECISION OD-2.
  E1 (RECOMMENDED; the form written in RF:49 before any score): per group g,
      y_hat = N x (1 + b_g x ONI),  ONI = x__oni (ONI of issue - 3, the engine's own lagged value), all months,
      b_g by least squares through the origin on the fit set (guardfit for the guard, refit for the test).
    Why faithful: Chen et al. predict a region's FIRE-SEASON total from an SST index months ahead, a regional linear
    regression. Summed over a region's unit-months, E1 gives total = N_total (1 + b_g ONI): exactly a regional linear
    regression anchored at climatology, spread over units and months in proportion to their normal, so the effect
    falls in the fire season (where N is large; the regressor N x ONI weights those rows). Per group, not per unit:
    ONI is one global series, so a unit slope would rest on ~16 independent years per unit-month; Chen et al. fit
    regions. ONI only (the plan names ONI; Chen 2011 also used Atlantic indices for the Amazon: not added).
  E2 (alternative): E1 restricted to the group's 3-month fire season (outside: the normal) - closer to "fire-season
    severity", and the same season rule as T1.
  E3 (alternative): one slope per group x calendar month b_{g,m} (all months; a month with < min_month_rows rows uses
    the group slope): lets the teleconnection change sign through the year; 12x the parameters on ~16 years each.

4.2 adapters/fire_turco.py: SPI-lag climate-fire model (Turco et al. 2018). OWNER DECISION OD-1.
  No functional form is written in any plan file (PEND :1004). Not verified against the paper text this session (no
  network): the forms below are faithful to its STRUCTURE as the plan states it (PLAN :281: "observed SPI at the issue
  month from panel precipitation"): fire-season burned area explained by SPI, the accumulation scale chosen on
  training data, used with observed (antecedent) SPI only. The owner (or the agent with network) should check the
  paper before signing.
  T1 (RECOMMENDED): per group g,
      fire season F_g (3 consecutive months, rule above, from the fit set's normals);
      on the fit rows in F_g with every scale valid: for each k in options.scales (SPI-1, 3, 6, 12, 24 at the issue
      month), b_k by least squares through the origin of y - N on N x SPI_k; SSE_k = sum (y - N (1 + b_k SPI_k))^2;
      k* = argmin SSE_k (ties -> the order of options.scales); then b_g = least squares on the fit rows in F_g with a
      valid SPI_k* (fallback rule above);
      y_hat = N x (1 + b_g x SPI_k*(issue)) inside F_g, N outside.
    The scale choice is part of the method's fit on training rows only (Turco selects the SPI accumulation from the
    data); it is not a pick on inner or guard rows. Sign free (dry antecedent -> more fire in fuel-rich regions,
    wet antecedent -> more fuel in fuel-limited ones).
  T2 (alternative, simplest): per group, all months, one fixed scale (x__spi_03m): y_hat = N (1 + b_g SPI_3).
  T3 (alternative, two antecedent terms, closest to the paper's two-predictor shape): inside F_g,
      y - N = b1 N SPI_3 + b2 N SPI_12 by least squares through the origin (2 x 2 normal equations; singular -> fallback),
      N outside F_g.
  Recommendation: T1 (keeps the paper's fire-season focus and its data-driven scale; one slope per region).
